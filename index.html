Hybrid Rig Tools — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

"Hybrid Rig Tools" is a private, single-user tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel @HybridRig to upload and schedule that channel's own videos, set thumbnails and captions, and read that channel's own statistics through the YouTube Data API.

What data is accessed

Only the owner's own YouTube channel data (videos, captions, thumbnails, playlists and statistics), accessed with the owner's own Google OAuth consent.

How it is used and stored

Data is used only to manage the owner's channel. OAuth tokens and statistics are stored locally on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Other users

The tool is not offered to the public and collects no data from anyone else.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
